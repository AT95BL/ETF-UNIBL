# 📚 ETF-UNIBL: Academic Archive

This repository serves as a comprehensive collection of my personal study notes, solved problems, and materials developed during my Software Engineering studies at the **Faculty of Electrical Engineering (ETF), University of Banja Luka**.

## 📂 Repository Structure

The archive is organized into specialized directories for various engineering disciplines:

**Mathematics & Theory:** Discrete Mathematics, Mathematical Analysis IV, Cryptography, and Data Security.
**Physics & Electronics:** Physics (OET1 & OET2), Basic Electronics, and Electrical Circuits.
**Systems & Performance:** Operating Systems Theory, Performance of Computer Systems, and Signals & Systems.
**Languages:** Technical English for Engineers.

## 🛠️ Purpose
The primary goal of this repository is to track my academic progress and provide a structured resource for exam preparation. 
It reflects a deep dive into fundamental engineering subjects, including **Fourier transforms, electromagnetics, and ANOVA testing**.
